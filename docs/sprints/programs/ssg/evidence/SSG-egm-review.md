## Protocol overview

Following `EGM - The Elephant-Goldfish Protocol.md`, isolated `egm-*` reviewers inspect the sprint suite with no shared conversation context and verified access to the codebase. Every reported omission or contradiction is cross-checked against repository source before acceptance or dismissal. Two complete EGM passes govern Program SSG.

1. **Round 1 (Initial `SPRINT-SSG-00..07` draft).** `8` isolated Goldfish reviewers produced `187` raw findings (`15` blockers, `114` majors, `50` minors, `8` questions), consolidated by `egm-synthesizer` into `9` root blockers (`B1..B9`) and `5` thematic major clusters.
2. **Round 2 (Upgraded StageShow-modeled + Bombadil-dashboard `SPRINT-SSG-00..08` suite).** Fresh `8`-agent isolated Goldfish pass followed by Stage 4 (`egm-zoom-out`) and Stage 5 (`egm-implementation-readiness`) verification over the edited documents.
